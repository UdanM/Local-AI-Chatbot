
University Examination Regulations

Exam Retakes:
Students who fail an examination may retake it up to two times. This means a student has a maximum of three attempts in total, including the original attempt.

Registration:
Students must register for an examination at least seven days before the examination date. Late registration is not normally accepted.

Passing Grades:
A student must achieve at least 50 percent to pass an examination. Some programmes may have additional requirements.

Absence:
Students who miss an examination without an approved reason are normally considered to have failed that attempt. Students with an approved medical or other valid reason should contact the examination office.

Appeals:
Students may submit an appeal against an examination result within ten working days after the result is announced.
